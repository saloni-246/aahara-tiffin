FAQ

Common questions

Can I order one tiffin without subscribing?

Yes. You can order a single tiffin or choose a recurring plan whenever you want.

Do you cook separate meals for every customer?

No. Aahara works from a practical weekly menu and matches suitable components and meal combinations to each customer profile.

How do allergies work?

Allergy information is treated as a higher priority than general preferences. Dishes with allergens can be clearly marked and filtered out when needed.

What if I want halal non-vegetarian meals?

When the weekly menu includes suitable halal non-veg dishes, those remain available to customers with halal + non-vegetarian preferences.
